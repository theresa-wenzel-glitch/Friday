# Client work

**Stub.** One of the three things that fill the working day, alongside
[[Content and audience]] and [[Research]]. Established during the setup
interview; see [[Setup interview 2026-08-13]].

## What is known

- Client and freelance work is a regular, recurring part of the week.
- Revenue runs through Stripe, so it is visible to the `metrics` skill.
- It is the pillar with **other people's deadlines attached**, which is why the `inbox` skill ranks "someone is blocked on your reply" above everything else, and why `plan` lets a client deadline take two of the day's three slots.

## What is not known

Who the clients are. How work arrives — inbound, referral, retainer, platform.
Typical engagement length and size. Whether invoicing is inside Stripe or
elsewhere. What a good month looks like in numbers.

None of this has been captured, and none of it should be guessed. Fill it in from
a real conversation or a real capture in `raw/`.

## Open questions

- Is there a rate card, or is each engagement priced fresh?
- Which parts of delivery are repetitive enough to be worth a skill of their own?
