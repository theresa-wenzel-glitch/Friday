# Content and audience

**Stub.** One of the three pillars of the working day, alongside [[Client work]]
and [[Research]]. Established during the setup interview; see
[[Setup interview 2026-08-13]].

## What is known

- Things get produced publicly, and how they land is watched.
- The measured surfaces are **Instagram and YouTube** — follower counts and per-post engagement flow into the `metrics` skill.
- Mentions and chatter about these topics are one of the three things `trends` watches.
- This pillar has **momentum rather than deadlines**, which is why `plan` protects a slot for it instead of letting client work absorb every day.

## What is not known

The subject matter. Publishing cadence. Whether the two channels carry the same
material or different material. Whether there is a newsletter — it was offered as
an option during setup and not selected, which suggests no, but that is an
inference and not a fact.

## Open questions

- Which single number actually indicates whether a piece worked? Follower count is the easiest to read and usually the least informative.
- Is there a backlog of ideas somewhere that should become a `raw/` capture?
