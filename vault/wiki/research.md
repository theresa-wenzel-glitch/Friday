# Research

**Stub.** The third pillar of the working day, alongside [[Client work]] and
[[Content and audience]]. Established during the setup interview; see
[[Setup interview 2026-08-13]].

## What is known

- Reading, following a field, and synthesising notes is a regular part of the week.
- It is fed by the `trends` skill, which watches AI and tech news, named competitor sites, and chatter on tracked topics.
- It has **neither deadlines nor momentum**, so it loses every contest against the other two pillars unless something defends it. That is the entire reason `plan` reserves a slot for it by default and says so out loud when a client deadline takes that slot.
- This is the pillar the `raw/` → `wiki/` pipeline exists for: captures land raw, and distilling them into topic pages *is* the research work.

## What is not known

The field or fields. Which sources are actually trusted versus merely followed.
Whether research feeds [[Client work]], [[Content and audience]], or is its own
end — the answer changes how `plan` should rank it.

## Open questions

- The `trends` skill needs a source list before it can run. That list belongs in this vault as its own page; it does not exist yet.
- What is the tell that a piece of research was worth the time — a note, a published thing, a decision changed?
